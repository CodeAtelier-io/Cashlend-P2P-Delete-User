Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Cashlend account and personal data

This page explains how to request the deletion of your Cashlend account and the personal data associated with it. The Cashlend app is provided by Cashlend BG OOD.

Before you delete your account

Your account can be deleted only when:

  • you have no active investments;
  • you have no unpaid investment installments;
  • your virtual wallet balance is zero — please withdraw your funds first.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Cashlend app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Request deletion by email

You can also request deletion without the app. Send an email to our support team from the email address registered to your account:

support@cashlend.bg

In your message, please include:

  • The subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  • The full name and email address linked to your Cashlend account

Sending the request from your registered email address helps us verify your identity and process your request faster.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete:

  • your sign-in credentials and profile settings;
  • your contact details (email address and phone number), except where they form part of the records listed below;
  • your notification preferences and marketing consents.

What is retained, and for how long

We are legally required to keep the following data after your account is closed:

  • Identity verification (KYC) data — names, national identification number, date of birth, address and copies of identity documents — for 5 years after the business relationship ends, under the Bulgarian Measures Against Money Laundering Act.
  • Transaction, contract and payment records, including your IBAN — for 5 years under the same act.
  • Accounting records — for 10 years under the Bulgarian Accountancy Act.

This data is stored securely, is not used for any other purpose, and is deleted when the retention period ends.

How long it takes

We aim to confirm and process valid deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify your identity.

Questions

For any questions about deleting your account or your data, contact us at info@cashlend.bg.
