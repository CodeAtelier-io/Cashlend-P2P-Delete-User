Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Cashlend account and personal data

This page explains how to request the deletion of your Cashlend account and the personal data associated with it.

Request deletion by email

If you would like your profile and personal data to be deleted, please send a request to our support team from the email address registered to your account:

support@cashlend.bg

In your message, please include:

  • The subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  • The full name and email address linked to your Cashlend account

Sending the request from your registered email address helps us verify your identity and process your request faster.

Delete your account from the app

You can also delete your account directly in the Cashlend mobile app:

  1. Open the Cashlend app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

What is deleted

When your request is completed, your account is closed and your personal data (such as your name, email, phone number, profile picture, and identity information) is deleted or anonymized.

What may be retained

Certain information must be kept for a limited period after account closure where we are legally required to retain it — for example, transaction and financial records that we must keep to comply with accounting, tax, and anti–money-laundering (AML) regulations. This data is securely stored and deleted once the legal retention period expires.

How long it takes

We aim to confirm and process valid deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify your identity.

Questions

For any questions about deleting your account or your data, contact us at info@cashlend.bg.
